Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1 Who We Are

TES (The Elite System) is a fitness business operating system developed and operated by Emmanuel Aneke. We provide software services to fitness businesses including gyms, personal training studios and martial arts centres. Our platform is accessible at tes-app-lac.vercel.app.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you sign up for TES, including your name, email address, business name, phone number and payment information. We also collect information about how you use the platform including pages visited, features used and actions taken. If you connect third-party services such as Stripe, Google Calendar or Twilio we receive data necessary to operate those integrations.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the TES platform, process payments, send transactional emails and SMS messages, provide customer support, and communicate important updates about your account. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

4 Your Clients' Data

When you use TES to manage your gym clients you are the data controller for your clients' information. TES acts as a data processor on your behalf. You are responsible for ensuring you have the appropriate legal basis to collect and process your clients' data. We store client data securely in Supabase, a GDPR-compliant cloud database provider.

5 Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) hosted on AWS infrastructure. Payment information is processed by Stripe and is never stored on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to authorised personnel only.

6 Third-Party Services

TES integrates with the following third-party services: Stripe for payment processing, Twilio for SMS messaging, Resend for email delivery, OpenAI for AI-powered features, Google for calendar and search integrations, and Supabase for database and authentication. Each service has their own privacy policy governing their use of data.

7 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. You may export all your data from Settings → Data & Privacy within the TES platform. To request account deletion or data removal contact us at support@tescrm.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8 Cookies

TES uses essential cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings but this may affect the functionality of the platform.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email. Continued use of TES after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10 Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests contact us at: support@tescrm.com
