FAQ

Common questions

Is TES a CRM or something else?+
TES stands for The Elite System — and it's more than a CRM. A CRM stores contacts. TES runs your business. It ships with a live branded app, processes payments directly to your bank, automates your marketing, monitors competitors, tracks client app usage, and runs 24/7 on its own. It's the operating system your gym runs on — not a tool you check occasionally.
Do my clients need to download a new app?+
Your clients download TES Trainer from the App Store or Google Play — the same app used by thousands of people. When they sign in, they see your gym's name, logo and brand colours. It looks and feels like your own app. No separate App Store submission required.
Zero transaction fees — how does that work?+
We use Stripe Connect. Your clients pay their membership fees and that money flows directly into your bank account — bypassing TES entirely. We charge you a flat $500/month for the platform. We never see your clients' payments. Every penny they pay goes to you.
How do Facebook and Instagram leads get into TES?+
Leads from Facebook Lead Ads and Instagram ads flow directly into your Kanban pipeline automatically — no manual work. Connect via Zapier (zero code needed) or our direct Facebook integration. Every new lead lands in "New" and fires a welcome automation immediately.
What's included in the 14-day free install?+
Full access to every module — no restrictions, no feature gates. Install your branded app layer, import your clients, connect Stripe, fire up your first automations. Most gym owners have TES fully running within 48 hours. No credit card required.
Can I migrate from my current platform?+
Yes. Download our CSV template, fill in your existing client data, and upload it. Clients are imported in minutes. We support migration from Mindbody, Trainerize, spreadsheets, and most other platforms. Enterprise plans include white-glove migration handled by our team.
